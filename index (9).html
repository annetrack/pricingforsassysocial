01 / The opportunity

Your offer deserves
a clear path to yes.

A strong offer needs more than a page that looks good. Prospective customers should quickly understand what you offer, why it matters to them, and exactly what to do next. The funnel will be planned around your audience, offer, and conversion goal—not a generic template.

01

The offer is hard to scan

If the value, deliverables, or next step are unclear, visitors may leave before understanding why your offer is right for them.

02

Too many competing actions

A focused funnel guides visitors toward one primary action instead of asking them to figure out where to go next.

03

Benefits need a clear story

Visitors need to connect their problem to your solution through a clear message, relevant proof, and specific benefits.

04

Friction before conversion

Unnecessary form fields, unclear booking steps, or missing reassurance can make it harder to take action.

05

Traffic without a clear next step

Social content, referrals, and campaigns work harder when they lead to a dedicated page with a specific conversion goal.

06

The journey should feel connected

From the first page visit to the confirmation message, each step should feel consistent and intentional.

02 / Proposed approach

Build around the offer,
not just the page.

We’ll map the conversion goal first, then shape the page flow, messaging hierarchy, calls to action, and form or booking step around that goal. Where included in the selected package, the funnel will connect to CRM and follow-up automation so new leads have a clear next step after submitting.

Primary objective

Create a conversion-focused funnel that explains the offer, builds confidence, and makes the next action obvious—supported by a practical lead capture and follow-up process where included in scope.

PHASE 03

Check mobile layouts, forms, links, and the conversion path; then walk through how to manage the funnel.
